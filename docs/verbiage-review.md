## Initial Review Notes

Likely review targets for sales/SEO cleanup:
- several sections still use operational or diagnostic phrasing that may sound internal rather than persuasive
- phrases like `narrow the right next step` and `operational drag` may need softer, more buyer-facing alternatives
- some quiz framing is still process-heavy instead of outcome-heavy
- sector and industry pages are stronger than before, but can still move closer to direct-market landing page language
- the lighter service pages still need the same rewrite depth as Payments and POS
